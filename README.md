Berlin-based software engineer. I build things, sometimes because someone's paying me to, sometimes because I got annoyed by something at 11pm and decided to fix it.

## What I'm building

- **[Smoketest](https://smoketest.sh)**: AI browser testing connected to Jira and Linear. Send a ticket to QA, let an agent test it in a real browser, and get the results back on the ticket.
- **[Nestornotes](https://nestornotes.com)**: A searchable knowledge hub for newsletters, articles, videos, and feeds, with collections, summaries, and personal digests. Web and iOS.
- **[IntelliScope](https://intelliscope.app)**: A workspace for scientific congress intelligence. Prioritize abstracts, coordinate coverage, and keep research, people, and notes connected beyond the conference.
- **[Allo](https://allo.arminayat.dev)**: A supervised speakerphone assistant for preparing calls, following captions and translations, and giving private instructions while staying in control.
- **Morrow** *(hardware prototype)*: A bedside companion exploring a more personal wake-up, with a voice, a little personality, and a reason to get up that's yours.
- **[Vera](https://vera.arminayat.dev)**: A journal that calls you. An AI companion that turns everyday phone conversations into a journal, with thoughtful questions and personal check-ins.
- **[Kapier](https://kapier.arminayat.dev)**: An iPhone app for understanding official letters and tracking possible actions and dates. A local-first document archive with explicit choices about cloud processing.
- **[Vinoh](https://vin-oh.com)**: A wine journal for remembering bottles and discovering your taste. Scan a label, save a tasting, and explore with an AI wine companion. [iOS app](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6738624509).
